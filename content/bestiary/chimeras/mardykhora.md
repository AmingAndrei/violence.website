Mardykhora (Sg. _Mardykhor_, _Mardykhor faevorus_) are a species of hyper-predatory Chimera.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Mardykhora have a body plan resembling a mix of large feline and baboon, are quadrupedal, and reach ~7 m in length. Their legs are very flexible and muscular, and their feet possess opposable thumbs and toes, along with sharp, retractile claws. Their fur is reddish-brown. Their muzzles are elongated, and have a baboon-leonine appearance, further compounded by the thick mane around their head. They possess a double set of jaws, one horizontal and one vertical, separating their head into four quadrants, each able to move independently from the others and each possessing a front-facing eye. Their tails are long and flexible, possessing spine-like hairs at the tip. The colour of both genders' Phlogiston is green.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Mardykhora are apex predators, and are the natural predator of Fae. They will stalk Fae groups for days at a time for the right moment. Despite being mostly solitary, they are highly emotive and communicative.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Mardykhora secrete an iron-based neurotoxin all throughout their body: glands in their skin coat their fur with it, the spines on their tail and their fangs inject it, and their breath constantly releases it. This toxin can induce shock within Fae in mere seconds, but is only mildly dangerous to other species, with deaths resulting from heavy metal poisoning as a result of overexposure, and is completely harmless to other Mardykhora.

The node density in a Mardykhor's throat allow it to spit Phlogiston out, usually as a series of multiple, high impact bursts. A second density is located on the tip of their tail, which can release Phlogiston directly into the spines, injecting it directly into their opponent. Their legs allow them to easily overpower beings smaller than themselves, all while remaining entirely silent, and their opposable thumbs and toes allow them to manipulate objects and climb with ease.
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Mardykhora reproduce sexually. They don't display any sexual dimorphism, and do not have any set mating season. Females lay 2-3 eggs/clutch, and the cubs will remain with their mother for the entirety of their upbringing, clinging to her back. The eggs will release a gaseous form of their neurotoxin if the egg shell is in any way damaged.
# .diet
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Mardykhora are omnivorous, although their diet is overwhelmingly carnivorous.
