Gargouilles (Fem. _Grotesques_, _Gargouilla pluvia_) are a species of sentinel Chimera.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gargouilles have a somewhat mammalian body plan, reaching ~14 m in length. They are quadrupeds, along with a pair of large wings. Their bodies are covered in a thick, mineralised hide, resembling stone to the touch. Their heads are bat-like in appearance, having short snouts with pronounced nasal cavities, and a pair of large, ear-like horns. Their wings are significantly larger than the rest of their limbs, so much so that they're used as primary front limbs when walking. Their tails are also significantly longer than their body, with a large fin going its entire length.

Grotesques are smaller, having a stockier build, reaching ~7 m. Their features are generally less pronounced, with their wings and tail more proportionate to their body.  The colour of both genders' Phlogiston is blue.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Both Gargouilles and Grotesques are mostly sedentary creatures. Their lifestyle involves entering long periods of brumation, resuming activity when the right conditions are met. Their hides can often become weathered from these long periods, and other lifeforms such as algae or moss can develop with ease.

Gargouilles will spend most of their lifetime solitary, guarding large territories their entire lives, attacking any creature that may appear as a threat and guarding those that it deems part of its territory. They are most active during rainy weather. Grotesques form colonies large colonies, living in underground cave systems. This is because compared to the hide of a Gargouille, a Grotesque hide is thicker, dries out faster and thus requires a much higher ambient humidity, without which it would turn brittle and cause painful gashes.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gargouilles have a specialised sack in their throat used to store water. A majority of their Phlogiston nodes are located in their throat, which they use in conjunction with the water to shoot hyper-pressurised jets, composed of both Phlogiston and boiling water, capable of reaching ~500.000 PSI. They use this jet both as a ranged attack and to excavate large chunks of rock. Their throats also possess a series of holes, through which the Gargouille can shoot out steam to whistle, using it as a primary method of communication.

The wings of a Gargouille hold the remaining majority of their node density. By using the stored water, Gargouilles can reach great flying speeds by using steam propulsion. Their tail feature a specialised set of gills extending its entire length, which, when submerged in water, will pump it directly into their throat sack, along with their hide, which has microscopic grooves which channel all water directly into their sack through capillary action.

Grotesques lack this throat sack and all adaptations relating to it. They are incapable of sustained flight, using their wings to glide short distances. They are, however, much stronger physically than Gargouilles, using their limbs to dig through solid rock. They can expel Phlogiston from their mouth out as a sticky substance. 
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gargouilles reproduce sexually. During mating season, multiple Gargouilles will court a Grotesque colony, working together to raise young, defend the colony, provide sustenance and expand the cave system. Grotesques lay 1-2 eggs/clutch. As side effect of the brumation, the average Gargouille life span is ~250 years.
# .diet
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gargouilles are omnivores, a large majority of their diet being comprised of plant matter and fish. Grotesques are also known to cultivate cave flora and fungi.
