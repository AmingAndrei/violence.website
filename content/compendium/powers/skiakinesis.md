Skiakinesis is a human-exclusive form of sorcery.
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis uses light as its main power source, without which it cannot be cast. It can only be taught by other sorcerers, with no way to properly transcribe it.
# .characteristic
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis involves the projection of shadows to affect the world around you.

The way it functions is that the sorcerer will send specific signals along their nerves which, when interfacing with direct with photons, will broadcast the effect onto their target. These signals will be interpreted and channeled with the sorcerer's hands, and using them requires the user to form shadow puppets.

Skiakinesis techniques fall into three categories: Receive You, YOUSEE, and Pierce Her. Most techniques require the use of both hands, although some only require one. Their effects are not purely external, as some directly affect the sorcerer's own body. The effects are also not purely magic, as they rely on certain physical and chemical traits of the target, with the broadcast signal interfering with these properties directly. The target also needs to be in contact with the same light source or the cast shadow, within a range of ~5 m.
# .limitations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis has the hard requirement of an active, powerful enough light source in the caster's vicinity, strong enough to cast a fully formed shadow. The efficiency of Skiakinesis is inversely proportional to local [[anima|Anima]] density, suffering from a massive dip in strength within the FLIPSIDE. 

Due to relying on the sorcerer's nervous structure, only regular humans with a healthy nervous system are capable of using Skiakinesis, excluding [[hybrids]], [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds, and people with neurodegenerative diseases.
