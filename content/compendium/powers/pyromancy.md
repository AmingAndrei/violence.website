Pyromancy is an anti-[[anima|Anima]] technique, unique to [[chimeras|Chimeras]].
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Pyromancy is entirely dependent on the presence of a [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]] soul, limiting its usage to only [[chimeras|Chimeras]] and [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds. All [[chimeras|Chimeras]] are born with some form of Pyromancy ingrained into them.
# .characteristic
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Pyromancy revolves around the application of [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]] and the control of the nodes and vessels around one's body. Greater control over these nodes can allow one to alter how the [[Phlogiston]] is released and how it behaves once separated from the soul. Pyromancy is as much about psychological training as it is about physiological training, as it is about learning to properly use Pyromancy tools, which can further enhance one's Pyromancy.
# .limitations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

As the main component of Pyromancy is [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]], all techniques are dependent on [[anima|Anima]], with its overall strength scaling with the local [[anima|Anima]] density. Overusing Pyromancy techniques can cause the local [[anima|Anima]] density to temporarily decrease, which will directly impact the efficiency of [[Phlogiston]] and of Pyromancy.
