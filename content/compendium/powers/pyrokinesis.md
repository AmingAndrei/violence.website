Pyrokinesis is a technique unique to [[chimeras|Chimeras]].
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Pyrokinesis is entirely dependent on the presence of a [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]] soul, limiting its usage to only [[chimeras|Chimeras]] and [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds. All [[chimeras|Chimeras]] are born with some form of Pyrokinesis knowledge ingrained into them.
# .characteristic
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Pyrokinesis revolves around the application of [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]] and the control of the nodes and vessels around one's body. Greater control over these nodes can allow one to alter where and how the [[Phlogiston]] is released, and how it behaves once outside the body. Some species have bodies more suited to one type of Pyrokinesis over another. 

Pyrokinesis is considered an anti-[[Anima]] technique, due to [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]]'s ability to incinerate [[anima|Anima]]. Mastering Pyrokinesis is as much about psychological training as it is about physiological training, learning to have better control over your own body. Pyrokinetic tools are tools and devices used in conjunction with or powered by Pyrokinesis, which are used to further enhance one's capabilities.
# .limitations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

As the main component of Pyrokinesis is [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]], all techniques are dependent on [[anima|Anima]] as fuel, with its overall strength scaling with the local [[anima|Anima]] density. Overusing Pyrokinesis techniques can cause the local [[anima|Anima]] density to temporarily decrease, which will directly impact the efficiency of [[Phlogiston]] and of Pyrokinesis. Overuse can also strain the body, which will also impact Pyrokinesis proficiency.
