The Nightmare Shackles are an [[absolutes|Absolute]], embodying the concept of containment.
# .design
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Nightmare Shackles resemble stone cubes, with a side length of ~10 cm. They are all composed of black stone, differing only by the colour of their sheen. There are three Nightmare Shackles: a Green, a Blue, and a Red one.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Created in the same process that birthed the [[supreme_acts_of_violence|Supreme Acts of Violence]] to act as counterbalance to their existence, the Nightmare Shackles transform into multiple chains, restraining the target they were pointed at. The amount of chains and the way they bind their target depends entirely upon the difficulty of restraining it. Once activated, a Nightmare Shackle can only be deactivated by the person that first activated it.

The chains will counter any attempt at breaking them by redirecting the energy into reinforcing themselves, shown through glowing in their respective colour, meaning struggle will only make them harder to break. They will keep the target in a form of virtual immortality and conscious suspended animation.

Currently, the Green Nightmare Shackle is in the [[pale|Pale]]'s possession, used to bind Violence Against God inside the Sarcophagus.
