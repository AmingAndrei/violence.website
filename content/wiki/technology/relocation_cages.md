Relocation cages are specialised enclosures used by [[ultraviolet|Ultraviolet]] when dealing with non-sapient [[daemons|Daemons]].
# .design
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Relocation cages come in all different sizes, depending on the threat. Their walls are ~10 cm thick and constructed from a high density, high melting point metal alloy. The upper regions of the cage have vents to allow for air circulation. Each is equipped with a terminal near the gate.
# .specs
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

All relocation cage are controlled through their terminal, encrypted to allow only trained personnel to interface with one. The only real control that matters is that of the main gate, operated through hydraulics, and the lights inside. Once the target is secured, the are transported far outside City Zero territory and released. While smaller cages can be transported by air, bigger ones come equipped with tracked treads, also controlled through the terminal.
