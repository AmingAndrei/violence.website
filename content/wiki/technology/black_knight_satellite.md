The Black Knight Satellite is an artificial satellite of [[mosaics|Mosaic]] origin orbiting the planet.
# .design
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Black Knight Satellite is cylindrical in shape and 8 m length, composed out of a black, matte material. It has little to no angled surfaces, instead possessing rounded edges. Its standout component is its lens, composed out a highly intricate framework of quartz filigrees. The lens' colour is a combination of splashes cyan, magenta, and yellow.
# .specs
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Black Knight Satellite is used by [[mosaics|Mosaics]] to quickly transport themselves out of the FLIPSIDE, and anywhere on the surface of the planet. The Satellite can make extremely precise adjustments to perfectly line up with its assigned coordinates, all on its own. The [[mosaics|Mosaic]] is then projected from one of the FLIPSIDE terminals, onto the surface, through the Satellite's lens. Transport is one-way, and only [[mosaics|Mosaics]] are able of using it.

The Black Knight Satellite cannot interact with areas within the Serpent's barrier, making direct transport within or near City Zero impossible, seen as a yellow blanket through the Satellite's lens.
