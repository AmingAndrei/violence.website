Fael Cores are devices at the base of most [[Fae]] machinery.
# .specs
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Fael Cores are, in essence, hyper-efficient, highly compact mechanical calculators, powered by [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]]. This fuel source directly imbues the machine with life, allowing it function and efficiency beyond what normal fuel could offer. Most machines of [[fae|Fae]] origin possess a Fael Core, acting as its main processing unit, its 'heart and brain'.

Smaller machinery, such as [[pyrokinesis|Pyrokinetic]] tools, can fuel directly from the user. Larger machines, such as [[fae_automata|Automata]], require regular, external fuelling. [[hellfire|Hellfire]] can be used as an alternative fuel, proving even more efficient than [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]], although Fael Cores cannot resist the increased output and causes them to go haywire.
