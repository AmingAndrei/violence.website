Fae Automata are autonomous machines of [[fae|Fae]] origin.
# .design
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Fae Automata do not have a standard body plan and measurements, instead depending on the role the specific Automaton was designed to fulfil. Although, most Automata are humanoid in stature. Each Automaton possesses a Fael Core.
# .specs
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The main component of each Automaton is its Fael Core, acting as the 'heart and brain' of the machine, and as a result all Automata run on [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]] as fuel. The complexity and abilities of an Automaton scales with its fuel requirements. Automata are generally built with modularity in mind, allowing for replacement and upgrading of parts when required.

After the disappearance of Faelinar, complex Automata became obsolete due to unsustainable fuel needs. Most Automata today have basic processing capabilities, instead relying on hard-wired scenarios. [[hellfire|Hellfire]] was attempted as a fuel source, increasing the Automaton's abilities drastically and even allowing them to do things outside of its model's behaviour, although it quickly resulted in the Automaton going haywire and attacking the [[Fae]].
