Sol is the home world of [[humans|humanity]]. It is an ocean world with a circumference of ~40,000 km, and for most of history it was at least somewhat humid and covered by vegetation. It was the third planet from the Sun, with a liquid core that generated a magnetosphere. It had a dynamic atmosphere, with different seasons, completing a full revolution every 365.25 days, and an average surface temperature of 14.76 °C. It has one natural satellite, the Moon.

After the Storm in 0 AAV/AV, the temperature of the planet dropped globally to -20 °C, which along with the [[toxin]], killed ~98% of all life on Sol. Its magnetosphere disappeared, along with its Sun and all other celestial bodies in its system and observable stars, with the exception of the Moon. Currently, most heat reaching the surface is either of geothermal origin, or provided by the many dormant [[circuit_titans|Circuit Titans]] inhabiting the planet. Almost all surviving [[humans]] live in City Zero.

Compared to the [[flipside]], Sol has a rather low [[anima|Anima]] resonance, making [[devils|Devils]] significantly weaker in this dimension. This effect is felt by other [[daemons|Daemons]] as well.
