Shadowcraft is a [[humans|human]]-exclusive form of sorcery, created by Rafa.
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Shadowcraft uses light as its main power source, without which it cannot be cast. All [[humans]] possess the capacity to use Shadowcraft through Rafa, although it needs special training to actually achieve anything meaningful. It can only be taught by other sorcerers, with no way to properly transcribe it.
# .characteristic
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Shadowcraft involves the projection of shadows to affect the world around you. Its theorised that this is to specifically manipulate reality through negation, although the online one with true knowledge of how the process works is Rafa. In steps:
1. The sorcerer will concentrate and form specific hand signs resembling shadow puppets. Each sign tunes the sorcerer to a specific property, while the act of forming them perfectly aligns and synchronises those signals into a single pattern.
2. The sorcerer’s hands block the light, casting a shadow, and the light will entangle with the sorcerer’s outline and the edge of their hands’ shadow. The synchronised signals bind to that edge. The sharper and more defined the shadow, the more securely the signals are held together, and the quality of the shadow is used to determine the range of the effect.
3. The specific signals will resonate with reality, subtracting the selected property or aspect from the target. Most effects also take into account the target’s physical and chemical properties to determine its full output. The target must always be in the sorcerer’s direct line of sight.

While most techniques require both hands, some require only one. Some techniques will also deliberately target the sorcerer, affecting their own body.
# .limitations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Shadowcraft requires an active light source in the caster’s vicinity. The stronger the light, the more defined the shadow cast by the sorcerer’s hands, and the greater the technique’s range.

The efficiency of Shadowcraft is inversely proportional to local [[anima|Anima]] resonance, suffering from a massive decrease in strength within the FLIPSIDE. [[kirlian_mimics|Kirlian Mimics]] cannot copy it. If Rafa dies, the ability to use Shadowcraft disappears with her.

Only regular [[humans]] are capable of using Shadowcraft, excluding [[hybrids]] (due to their [[devils|Devil]] attractor effect being cell-deep and blocking all Shadowcraft), and people with neurodegenerative/motoric diseases (they can't synchronise their body enough for Shadowcraft to trigger). [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds can use Shadowcraft.

Each technique has a cooldown caused by the refractory period of the nervous system, and using the same technique without waiting that refractory period will apply the effect to both the target and the sorcerer.
