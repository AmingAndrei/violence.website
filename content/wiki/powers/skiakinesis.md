Skiakinesis is a human-exclusive form of sorcery, created by Rafa.
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis uses light as its main power source, without which it cannot be cast. All humans possess the capacity to use Skiakinesis thanks to Rafa, although it needs special training to actually achieve anything meaningful. It can only be taught by other sorcerers, with no way to properly transcribe it.
# .characteristic
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis involves the projection of shadows to affect the world around you, specifically interacting with the reality phenomenon of negation. In steps:
1. The sorcerer will concentrate on their goal and will form specific hand signs resembling shadow puppets. Each sign tunes the sorcerer’s neural signals to a specific property, while the act of forming them perfectly aligns and synchronises those signals into a single pattern.
2. The sorcerer’s hands block the light, casting a shadow, and the light will entangle with the sorcerer’s outline and the edge of their hands’ shadow. The synchronised signals bind to that edge. The sharper and more defined the shadow, the more securely the signals are held together, and the quality of the shadow is used to determine the range of the effect.
3. The specific neural signals will resonate with reality, subtracting selected properties and aspects from the target. Most effects also take into account the target’s physical and chemical properties to determine its full output. The target must always be in the sorcerer’s direct line of sight.
# .limitations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis requires an active light source in the caster’s vicinity. The stronger the light, the more defined the shadow cast by the sorcerer’s hands, and the greater the technique’s range.

The efficiency of Skiakinesis is inversely proportional to local [[anima|Anima]] resonance, suffering from a massive decrease in strength within the FLIPSIDE. If Rafa dies, the ability to use Skiakinesis disappears with her.

Only regular humans with a healthy nervous system are capable of using Skiakinesis, excluding [[hybrids]] (whose neural structure is too different), and people with neurodegenerative diseases. [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds can use Skiakinesis.

Each technique has a cooldown caused by the refractory period of the nervous system, and using the same technique without waiting that refractory period will apply the effect to both the target and the sorcerer.
