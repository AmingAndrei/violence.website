Skiakinesis is a human-exclusive form of sorcery, created by Rafa.
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis uses light as its power source, without which it cannot be cast. While the potential exists in every single human, thanks to the presence of Rafa's cells, it needs special training to actually achieve anything meaningful, and it can only be taught by other sorcerers, with no way to properly transcribe it.
# .characteristic
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis involves the projection of shadows to affect the world around you.

The way it functions is that the sorcerer will send specific signals along their nerves, which will then interact with the Rafa cells inside their body, sending and triggering the effect onto the target, at all times in the sorcerer's direct line of sight. Proper casting requires the sorcerer to form shadow puppets with their hands.

Most techniques require the use of both hands, although some only require one. Their effects are not purely external, as some directly affect the sorcerer's own body and the Rafa cells inside them. The effects are also not magic, as they rely on certain physical and chemical traits of the target, which the sorcerer is interacting with directly. The intensity of the light determining the range.
# .limitations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis has the hard requirement of an active, powerful enough light source in the caster's vicinity, strong enough to cast a fully formed shadow. The efficiency of Skiakinesis is inversely proportional to local [[anima|Anima]] resonance, as increased resonance jamming the [[anima|Anima]] signal required by Rafa's cells, suffering from a massive dip in strength within the FLIPSIDE. If Rafa is to perish, the [[anima|Anima]] signal required for her cells to function as a whole will cease, and so will Skiakinesis.

Due to relying on the sorcerer's nervous structure, only regular humans with a healthy nervous system are capable of using Skiakinesis, excluding [[hybrids]] (whose neural structure cannot interact with Rafa's cells at all), [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds (whose [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]] souls jams the signal to Rafa's cells due to it's unique interaction with [[anima|Anima]]), and people with neurodegenerative diseases. Each technique has a cooldown caused by the refractory period of the nervous system, and using the same technique without waiting that refraction period will apply the effect to both the target and the sorcerer.
