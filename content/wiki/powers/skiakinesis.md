Skiakinesis is a human-exclusive form of sorcery, created by Rafa.
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis uses light as its main power source, without which it cannot be cast. All humans possess Rafa cells and thus the capacity to use Skiakinesis, although it needs special training to actually achieve anything meaningful. It can only be taught by other sorcerers, with no way to properly transcribe it.
# .characteristic
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis involves the projection of shadows to affect the world around you. In steps:
1. The sorcerer will send specific signals along their nervous system, which will be intercepted by the Rafa cells inside their body. These signals require both mental focus, intent, and the formation of specific hand signs resembling shadow puppets.
2. The Rafa cells will absorb the light directly cast upon the sorcerer's hands. The quality of the shadow is used to determine the range of the effect.
3. The Rafa cells will modify the local [[anima|Anima]] resonance to enforce the desired effect upon the target. Most effects do not rely on [[anima|Anima]] alone, and instead also take into account the target's physical and chemical properties to determine its full output. The target must always be in sorcerer's direct line of sight.
# .limitations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Skiakinesis requires an active light source in the caster’s vicinity. The stronger the light, the more defined the shadow cast by the sorcerer’s hands, and the greater the technique’s range.

The efficiency of Skiakinesis is inversely proportional to local [[anima|Anima]] resonance, since the Rafa cells must overpower the resonance they are trying to alter, suffering from a massive dip in strength within the FLIPSIDE. If Rafa dies, her cells will cease to exist across all of humanity, and Skiakinesis will end with them.

Only regular humans with a healthy nervous system are capable of using Skiakinesis, excluding [[hybrids]] (whose neural structure cannot interact with the Rafa cells at all), [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds (whose [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]] soul destroys the [[anima|Anima]] output of the Rafa cells), and people with neurodegenerative diseases (whose nerves can no longer send the precise required signals to the Rafa cells).

Each technique has a cooldown caused by the refractory period of the nervous system, and using the same technique without waiting that refractory period will apply the effect to both the target and the sorcerer.
