Pyrokinesis is composed of various techniques unique to [[chimeras|Chimeras]].
# .overview
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Pyrokinesis is entirely dependent on the presence of a [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]] soul, limiting its usage to only [[chimeras|Chimeras]] and [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds. All [[chimeras|Chimeras]] are born with some form of Pyrokinesis knowledge ingrained into them.
# .mechanics
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Pyrokinesis revolves around the application of [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]] and the control of the nodes and vessels around one's body. Greater control over these nodes can allow one to alter where and how the [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]] is released, and how it behaves once outside the body. Some types have bodies more suited to one type of Pyrokinesis over another. 

Pyrokinesis is considered an anti-[[anima|Anima]] technique, due to [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]]'s ability to incinerate even the [[anima|Anima]] inside one's body.

Mastering Pyrokinesis is as much about psychological training as it is about physiological training, learning to have better control over your own body. 
# .pyrokinetic_tools
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Pyrokinetic tools are tools and devices used in conjunction with or powered by Pyrokinesis, which are used to further enhance one's capabilities.
# .limitations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

As the main component of Pyrokinesis is [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]], all techniques are dependent on [[anima|Anima]] as fuel, with their overall strength scaling with the local [[anima|Anima]] resonance. Overuse strains the body.
