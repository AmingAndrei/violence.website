Chromic Rot is a [[hybrids|hybrid]]-spread [[curses|curse]].
# .origin
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Chromic Rot originates from [[hybrids]] and usage of their transformation. As it is understood, overuse will imprint on the wider [[anima|Anima]] resonance a variable that, when intercepted by a member of the [[hybrids|hybrid]]'s type, fails to be interpreted, causing it to corrupt and Chromic Rot to spontaneously manifest inside their body.

The host will always be selected at random from the entire population of the [[hybrids|hybrid]]'s type. Other [[hybrids]] and [[revolutions|Revolutions]] are immune to its effects. Its name was coined by the [[chromacy|Chromacy]], choosing to interpret it as a punishment by Lady Chroma for the continued existence of [[hybrids]].
# .effects
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Chromic Rot manifests as a pale, rainbow infection within the host's body. As it progresses, this infection will spread throughout their entire body, slowly destroying their organs. The first signs of the [[curses|curse]] appear as painful coughing fits, and as it progresses the veins on a host will turn rainbow.

Chromic Rot is incurable, although treatments to alleviate the symptoms or slow down its progression do exist. Most hosts die from complications. The [[chromacy|Chromacy]] and other anti-[[hybrids|hybrid]] groups cite Chromic Rot as a reason to have them outcast or euthanised.
