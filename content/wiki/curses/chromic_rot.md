Chromic Rot is a [[hybrids|hybrid]]-spread curse.
# .origin
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Chromic Rot originates from [[hybrids]] and usage of their failsafe. Overuse will imprint on the wider [[anima|Anima]] resonance a variable which, when interpreted by a regular member of the [[hybrids|hybrid]]'s type, will cause Chromic Rot to spontaneously manifest inside their body.

The host is always random, and other [[hybrids]] and [[revolutions|Revolutions]] are immune to its effects. Its named was coined by the [[chromacy|Chromacy]], choosing to interpret it as a punishment by Lady Chroma for the continued existence of [[hybrids]].
# .effects
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Chromic Rot manifests as a pale, rainbow infection within the host's body. As it progresses, this infection will spread throughout their entire body, slowly destroying their organs. The firsts signs of the curse appear as painful coughing fits, and as it progresses the veins on a host will turn rainbow.

Chromic Rot in incurable, although treatments to alleviate the symptoms or slow down its progression do exist. Most hosts die from complications. The [[chromacy|Chromacy]] and other anti-[[hybrids|hybrid]] groups cite Chromic Rot as a reason to have them outcast or euthanised.
