The Blue Rose is a curse caused by Violence Against Nature.
# .origin
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Blue Rose's are embedded in all attacks done by Violence Against Nature, direct or through tools used by her.
# .effects
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Blue Rose is, at its simplest, Violence Against Nature's ability to shatter spatial reality. 

In terms of effects toward other beings, and especially [[devils|Devils]], the wounds inflicted by Violence Against Nature cannot be healed, as the spatial existence of the body part stops existing. A wound inflicted by Violence Against Nature on a [[devils|Devil]] will never be able to be regenerated, repaired, or replaced, ever.

Violence Against Nature can also use the effects of the Blue Rose on space itself, punching holes through and smashing spatial reality into shards. These fractures can be used as a weapon or as short-lived portals, as the shards will reform themselves after a short period.
