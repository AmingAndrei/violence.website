The Signal Stations are a network of eight relay towers positioned far outside [[city_zero|City Zero]].
# .design
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## …/tower

Each Signal Station is a 300 m broadcast tower with a sealed habitation area at its base, housing the Station's electronic systems and a filtration system that scrubs the [[toxin]] from air continuously drawn in from outside.
## …/technician

Each Station is staffed at all times by one technician, working six-month shifts. A technician operates and maintains their Station's systems and is responsible for confirming anything detected by it. Technicians also cross-check calibration against the readings of adjacent sectors, and perform a daily check on the technicians at adjacent Stations to confirm they remain safe and alive, notifying the [[pale|Pale]] Executive of any irregularities.
# .function
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## …/radar

The Signal Stations function as a radar network, capable of detecting anything approaching [[city_zero|City Zero]]. A detection must be confirmed by the assigned technician before [[ultraviolet|Ultraviolet]] is dispatched to intercept.
## …/coordinate_relay

The Station network is used for semi-local geolocation outside [[city_zero|City Zero]], by calculating the signal delay between different Stations.
## …/barrier_amplifier

The Signal Stations amplify the Serpent's barrier, extending its range far beyond [[city_zero|City Zero]]'s borders.
