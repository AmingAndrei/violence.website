City Zero is a 7,125 km² metropolis that appeared over the ruins of [[rojachert|Rojachert]] when Violence Against Art accidentally transported the Spine during the [[devil_parade|Parade]], and acts as the last [[humans|human]] settlement on [[sol|Sol]].
# .design
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## …/layout

The city is organised radially around the Spine, which is located at its centre. Beneath the city lies its foundation, with the [[signal_stations|Signal Stations]] located far outside its walls. Its architecture is characterised by white, simplistic buildings, with geometric protrusions extending from the facades of buildings. It manifested with fully functional infrastructure and furnishings.
## …/materials

The city and everything that appeared with it are made from a combination of crystallised carbon, silicon, and solid light, acting as a form of solid hologram, essentially a form of [[mosaics|Mosaic]]. Some of the surviving buildings from [[rojachert|Rojachert]] are now considered historical landmarks.
# .control
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

City Zero is jointly governed by the [[pale|Pale]], [[ultraviolet|Ultraviolet]], and the [[chromacy|Chromacy]].
# .infrastructure
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## …/damage_reversal

Whenever the city suffers damage, it will quickly revert to its original state, with damaged structures snapping back into place. The buildings of [[rojachert|Rojachert]] are unaffected by the damage-reversal.
## …/expansion

Whenever the city requires additional infrastructure, its projection radius can be expanded from within the Spine. This causes the wall to extend outwards, while new buildings and other structures manifest automatically. These additions are subject to the same damage-reversal properties as the rest of the city.
## …/life_support

City Zero is equipped with an extremely efficient air-filtration system. A network of pumps extracts the [[toxin]] from the surrounding air, filters it, and stores it within the city's foundation before pumping clean air onto the streets. The system is so effective that citizens can walk outside completely safely while within the city's borders.
## …/barrier

The city and its surrounding area are permanently covered by a barrier maintained by the Serpent. Its primary purpose is to prevent Violence Against Art from physically approaching the city. Should he somehow manage to pass through it, the barrier would prevent him from using his powers. As a side effect, it also prevents the [[black_knight_satellite|Black Knight Satellite]] from projecting onto the area.
## …/energy_production

All of City Zero's energy is produced by the Sarcophagus. Should it fail, the air-filtration system and expansion system would cease functioning immediately, and any damage sustained by the city would become permanent.
