Revolutions are special [[humans]], born as a reaction to immensely violent events, physically manifesting from [[anima|Anima]] itself. The degree of violence necessary for a Revolution to be born scales with the type's population. 

Revolutions represent the best of [[humans|humanity]], embodying all of their strengths with none of their weaknesses. They have genius level intellect, a perfect immune system, lack all genetic markers which could make them more susceptible to different conditions, and have an enhanced physical endurance, capable of surviving well into biological extremes which would kill a normal [[humans|human]]. They are also immune to the [[toxin]], [[gordian_gorgons|Gordian Gorgon]] infection, [[clutch_beetles|Clutch Beetle]] parasitism, and [[chromic_rot|Chromic Rot]].

The caveat to their existence is that their life is fleeting. As they age, their bodies begin breaking down, and they begin coughing more and more of their insides, liquified into a black, rainbow iridescent substance, the same substance their [[blood]] is composed off. The condition is fatal, and most Revolutions die by 30.

Revolutions are a [[humans|human]]-exclusive phenomenon, due to the higher thought necessary. Due to their unique relation to [[anima|Anima]], [[daemons|Daemons]] cannot birth Revolutions.
