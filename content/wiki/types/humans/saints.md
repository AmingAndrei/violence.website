Saints are special [[humans]], born as a reaction to immensely violent events, physically manifesting from [[anima|Anima]] itself. The degree of violence necessary for a Saint to be born scales with the type's population. 

Saints represent the best of humanity, embodying all of their strengths with none of their weaknesses. They have genius level intellect, a perfect immune system, lack all genetic markers which could make them more susceptible to different conditions, and have an enhanced physical endurance, capable of surviving well into biological extremes which would kill a normal human. They are also immune to the [[toxin]], [[gordian_gorgons|Gordian Gorgon]] infection, [[clutch_beetles|Clutch Beetle]] parasitism, and Chromic Rot.

The caveat to their existence is that their life is fleeting. As they age, their bodies begin breaking down, and they begin coughing more and more of their insides, liquified into a black, rainbow iridescent substance. The condition is fatal, and most Saints die by 30.

Saints are theoretically not a [[humans|human]]-exclusive phenomenon, as other types can also give birth to their own Saint-like beings. Due to their unique relation to [[anima|Anima]], [[angels|Angels]] and [[daemons|Daemons]] cannot be Saints.
