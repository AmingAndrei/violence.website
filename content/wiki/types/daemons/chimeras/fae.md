Fae (_Fae sapiens_) are a type of humanoid Chimera.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Fae have a humanoid body plan, reaching ~2 m in height. They have bluish-grey skin, covered with patches of very fine scales, possess large, circular eyes, digitigrade legs, and sharp, elongated ears. Their body is hairless, with the exception of their scalp, which grows similar to [[humans]]. The colour of their [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]] is red.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Fae are sapient, and highly social, forming large, highly industrialised cities and settlements, along the rims of craters, sinkholes, volcanoes caldera, and general circular depressions in the ground, with a highly complex and confusing legislature, anticipating every possible edge-case scenario. The lingua franca of Fae is Faevael. Their natural predators are [[mardykhora|Mardykhora]].
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## + [[pyrokinesis|Pyrokinesis]]

Fae have a greatest [[pyrokinesis|Pyrokinesis]] mastery of all Chimeras. Whenever using [[pyrokinesis|Pyrokinesis]], a Fae's hair will begin burning, although this doesn't damage it.
## - Iron weakness
The greatest weakness Fae have is towards iron. Direct contact with elemental iron releases certain ions that disrupt their ability to release [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]], along with draining them of all energy and strength. The system returns to normal not long after contact is disrupted.
## - Weak regeneration
Fae have the weakest regeneration of all Chimeras, capable of healing only shallow, surface-level wounds.
## - Credulity
The Fae neural structure is wired in such a way that makes outright lying an extremely taxing task, meaning they naturally prefer lying by omission. This also makes them extremely gullible, as they haven't developed the mental defences for lying. 
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Fae reproduce sexually, giving birth to live young, which are nursed for ~2 years. The gestation period is ~9 months, and full sexual maturity is reached in ~18 years, after which the aging rate slows drastically. The average Fae life span is ~120 years, with only a minimal decrease in performance.

Fae are capable of reproducing with [[humans]]. The result is a half-breed, anatomically identical to a [[humans|human]], including vulnerability to the [[toxin]], with the exception of a [[divine_flame|Divine Flame]] soul, slower aging, higher muscle strength, and almost total immunity to the FLIPSIDE's negation effect, with the only side effect being a mild feeling of nausea while inside the dimension.

# .diet
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Fae are omnivorous, with a slightly higher metabolism than [[humans]].
