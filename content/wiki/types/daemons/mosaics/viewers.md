Viewers (_Homo sapiens_) are [[Mosaics]] created from [[humans]].
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Viewers have a shadowy, humanoid appearance. They are completely black and featureless, with the exception of their glowing, animated white eyes.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Viewers like to watch. They gravitate around Violence Against Art.
# .weaknesses
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## …/spectators

Viewers cannot communicate, are completely intangible and cannot interact in any meaningful way with the world.

Normally, Viewers are completely invisible, even to Violence Against Art, although he has the ability to make them visible at will. Viewers created from [[revolutions|Revolutions]] can partially oppose this, as they while Violence Against Art can force them back into invisibility, they will always remain visible to him.
# .production
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Viewers are created when a [[humans|human]] is personally killed by Violence Against Art. They retain their minds and memories.
