Vespers (_Mosaica vespera_) are the most diverse type of [[mosaics|Mosaic]].
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Vespers have no set anatomy. Each individual vary in appearance, possessing an often abstract body plan. Their colouration often borders on the magenta spectrum. Usually, they will possess body plans and anatomical traits found in [[devils|Devils]].

The entire type is incidental, born as a side effect of Violence Against Art's existence, which left behind and permitted the refraction and multiplication of specific light frequencies that crystallised under [[devils|Devil]] influence, creating pseudo-[[devils|Devils]] that later developed into Vespers.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Vespers are self-aware and extremely intelligent. Their speech has the [[devils|Devil]] property of being automatically translated in the listener's mother language. Despite having the capacity for extreme violence, they are otherwise mostly cooperative and social.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## …/photon_propulsion
Vesper cells can expel infrared light for movement, using the momentum of the photons to reach extreme speeds. The process is done on an individual cellular level, with each cell negating air friction and having the ability to instantaneously switch emission direction, granting them extremely precise movement, bordering on teleportation. 
## …/light_sailing
They can use surrounding light sources for transport. This restricts their direction of travel to that of the light source, along with limiting their range to the light's intensity, although it is easier to master and less mentally exhausting than infrared emission.
## …/electric_current_movement
Vespers can use strong electric currents as a means of transport, able to use cables or electric arcs to transpose their entire bodies. 
## …/electronic_manipulation
They can inhabit electronic devices as long as they have a current actively running through them, and can even alter the currents in such a way they can control the device from inside.
## …/[[vectors]]

[[vectors|Vectors]] are powers native to Vespers, allowing them to alter the behaviour of their cells and granting them various abilities.
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Vespers reproduce through light. Certain imperceptible frequencies and wavelengths of light resulting from Vesper existence, in the presence of a high enough [[anima|Anima]] resonance, will crystallise into a womb-like larva. This larva will then absorb the surrounding [[anima|Anima]] and radiation, eventually reaching a point of saturation which will create a mature Vesper. If the Serpent interrupts and corrupts the womb, it will create a [[somas|Soma]].
