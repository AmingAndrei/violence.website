Somas (_Mosaica soma_) are a type of humanoid* Mosaics.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Somas are composed out of two distinct components, a Heart and a Shell.

The Heart of a Soma is the true organism, resembling a yellow slug-larva-thing. They are completely helpless, can barely move on their own, cannot speak, and have very weak senses.

The Shell is a humanoid construct piloted by the Heart. They are 1.9 m in height, have skin made out of a white, flexible, metallic alloy, have silver hair, and have animated screen eyes, displaying an animated yellow iris. Their faces are capable of displaying the full extent of [[humans|human]] emotion. They were designed by the Serpent to act as a scaffold for mutations and to bear resemblance to both [[humans]] and [[angels|Angels]].
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Somas are created by the Serpent as bodies to house itself in. They are corrupted [[vespers|Vespers]], although they lack pretty much all abilities and traits associated with [[vespers|Vespers]]. The Shells were implemented as a last-ditch solution to counteract the defects Somas possess and to act as a scaffold for the Serpent's abilities. They are social and cooperative, from an outside perspective having very little differences to [[humans]].
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## + Shell empowerment
The Heart takes control of a Shell by being placed inside its chest cavity. From there, the Heart will spread a nervous filament throughout the entire chassis, taking direct control of the motoric and sensory functions of the Shell, which from this point acts as a direct extension of its body. The Shell grants Somas enhanced mobility and senses, and the ability to speak.
## + Vexers
Vexers are an extremely rare anomaly where Somas which retain the [[vespers|Vesper]] ability of using [[vectors|Vectors]], directly caused by the Serpent's failure to fully override the [[vespers|Vesper]] code. With enough training, Vexers can gain access to other [[vespers|Vesper]] abilities.
## - Shell damage extension
Damage to the Shell will resonate and damage the Heart, which can kill it.
## - Shell integration
While they can be upgraded and repaired, Shells cannot be reliably swapped, as the control method means the Heart becomes integrated inside one, making the transplant extremely complicated and dangerous, and causing extreme discomfort to the Soma while it is forced to adapt. 
## - Shell senses localisation
Most sensors and motoric controls are located in the head, which means Somas can enter a catatonic state if their head is injured or severed. 
## - Heart weak-point
And damage to the Heart and chest area is almost always fatal.
# .mutations
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Soma mutations are the Serpent's final step in creating a powerful enough vessel for itself. A mutation is triggered when the Serpent manages to take full control of a Soma. The most common occurrence of this happening is when a Soma 'partially' dies, or dying without their Heart fully shattering, which, if the Serpent has enough influence, can force it to reform and bring the Soma back to life. In some cases, mutations can be forcefully triggered on a living Soma, such as by other mutated Somas. If a Soma is killed by Violence Against Art, they cannot mutate.

Biologically, the Soma's cells will begin forming organic structures inside the Shell, including functional, albeit awfully tasting [[blood]]. This process will start with the Heart's outer structure and use the Shell as a scaffold, progressing until the Soma's entire body, will turn organic, becoming a visual match to [[humans]]. Mentally, the Soma's mind will slowly be eroded and replaced/taken over by the Serpent, causing all sorts of delusions and depersonalisations, until all that is left is a fully functional body for the Serpent to use.

Physical mutations cannot be slowed down, but mental ones can, with certain looping and grounding exercises allowing the Soma to retain some control of themselves, though they become less effective with time. As mutation progresses, Somas will grow exponentially stronger and gain access to the abilities and [[vectors|Vector]] of the Serpent, although using them only accelerates mutation. Despite being organic, they remain bound by Soma biology, such as having no taste (despite having taste buds), being unable to sleep, or being able to be killed only if their Heart is destroyed (all other damage will be painful but not fatal and can actually cause the mutation to accelerate).
# .production
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Somas are created when a [[vespers|Vesper]] womb is corrupted by the Serpent. Instead of following its intended development, the Serpent injects itself as the base code, inadvertently forcing the womb into a state of permanent neoteny, eventually creating a Soma Heart.
