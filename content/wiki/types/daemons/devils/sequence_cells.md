Sequence Cells (_Diavolos strepitator_) are a type of obstreperous Devil.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The body of a Sequence Cell is composed entirely out of sound waves. They have no set size, although their average adult size is ~1.2 m in diameter. While they themselves are invisible, their outline can be seen through the anchor objects around them.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells are very primitive organisms, operating mostly out in reaction to outside stimuli. Most are not even all that hard to kill; the real difficulty of Sequence Cells is their extreme reproductive speed, as their environment can lead to them multiplying endlessly. They can be noticed from a distance through the unique sound they always emit, and their presence creates shapes in dust and sand.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

## + Anchoring
Sequence Cells must bind themselves to solid objects for their form to remain stable. These anchor objects will then be used as amplifiers, incorporated into a shifting sequences formed around the 'body'. These objects are usually similar or preferably even identical to each other, and their movement and shapes are determined by the individual Sequence Cell and their activity. Consuming [[blood]] allows the Sequence Cell to reform its configuration and quickly anchor itself to other objects.
## + Sound manipulation
Sequence Cells interact with their environment by manipulating sound, creating pressure differentials and kinetic force to manipulate objects or to move themselves.
## + Tool creation
They can absorb objects from their surroundings into themselves and apply a partial amplifier effect to them, enforcing a similar sequential arrangement to the objects and using them to defend themselves or attack others. These constructs have a very limited life span.
## + Size manipulation
They can expand or shrink by up to 30% their size when necessary. 
## + Senses
They sense their environment through sound and temperature, as temperature modifies the way sound waves move though a medium.
## + Liquid strengthening
Sequence Cells can inhabit liquid media such as water, inside which their strength is greatly elevated, becoming visible to the naked eye through the wave distortions they produce, and allowing them to survive with significantly fewer anchors.
## - Anchor dependency
One way of killing a Sequence Cell is destroying its anchor, which disrupts the configuration which it maintain its shape. The number and complexity of these anchors scales with the Sequence Cell's size, and a Sequence Cell's size is limited by the availability of anchors. After death or injury, red dust accumulated from the constant vibration is released from the surface of their anchors.
## - Solid barriers
They cannot pass through solid matter.
## - Sound overpowering weakness
Another method of killing a Sequence Cell is by overpowering its frequencies by creating an even louder noise source. The smaller and weaker the Sequence Cell the easier this is to do. In a liquid medium, this becomes much easier.
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells reproduce by division. When a Sequence Cell hits a jagged surface, its sound waves will be forcefully split in two, creating two smaller individuals, each inheriting half of the parent's size and anchors. Sequence Cells can voluntarily divide (but have no instinct prompting them to, and so never do), and can even fuse, with multiple Sequence Cells merging into a new, unique individual, with no upper size limit and inheriting all of the smaller Sequence Cells' anchors.
# .diet
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells feed on sound, specifically on rhythmic sounds. Small Sequence Cells will absorb the sound and use it to increase their size. They consume [[blood]] through acoustic atomisation.
