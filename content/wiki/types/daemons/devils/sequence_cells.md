Sequence Cells (_Diavolos strepitator_) are a type of obstreperous Devil.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The body of a Sequence Cell is composed entirely out of sound waves. They have no set size, although their average adult size is ~1.2 m in diameter. While they themselves are invisible, their outline can be seen through the anchor objects around them.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells are very primitive organisms, operating mostly out in reaction to outside stimuli. Most are not even all that hard to kill; the real difficulty of Sequence Cells is their extreme reproductive speed, as their environment can lead to them multiplying endlessly. They can be notices from a distance through the unique sound they always emit, and their presence creates shapes in dust and sand.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells must bind themselves to solid objects for their form to remain stable. These anchor objects will then be used as amplifiers, incorporated into a shifting sequences formed around the 'body'. These objects are usually similar or preferably even identical to each other, and their movement and shapes are determined by the individual Sequence Cell and their activity. The number and complexity of these anchors scales with the Sequence Cell's size, and a Sequence Cell's size is limited by the availability of anchors.

Sequence Cells interact with their environment by manipulating sound, creating pressure differentials and kinetic force to manipulate objects or to move themselves. They can expand or shrink by up to 30% their size when necessary. They sense their environment through sound and temperature, as temperature modifies the way sound waves move though a medium.

They can absorb objects from their surroundings into themselves and apply a partial amplifier effect to them, enforcing a similar sequential arrangement to the objects and using them to defend itself or attack others. These constructs have a very limited life span, and their creation doesn't impact the Sequence Cell itself.

Killing a Sequence Cell requires either overpowering its frequencies by creating an even louder noise source (the smaller and weaker the Sequence Cell the easier to do so), or by destroying its anchors and thus disrupting the configuration, without which it cannot maintain its shape and dies. Consuming [[blood]] allows the Sequence Cell to reform its configuration and quickly anchor itself to other objects. After death or injury, red dust accumulated from the constant vibration is released from the surface of their anchors.

They cannot pass through solid matter, but can inhabit liquid media such as water, inside which their strength is greatly elevated, becoming visible to the naked eye through the wave distortions they produce, and allowing them to survive with significantly fewer anchors. The downside is that sound overpowering a much bigger threat.
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells reproduce by division. When a Sequence Cell hits a jagged surface, its sound waves will be forcefully split in two, creating two smaller individuals, each inheriting half of the parent's size and anchors. Sequence Cells can voluntarily divide (but have no instinct prompting them to, and so never do), and can even fuse, with multiple Sequence Cells merging into a new, unique individual, with no upper size limit and inheriting all of the smaller Sequence Cells' anchors.
# .diet
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells feed on sound, specifically on rhythmic sounds. Small Sequence Cells will absorb the sound and use it to increase their size. They consume [[blood]] through acoustic atomisation.
