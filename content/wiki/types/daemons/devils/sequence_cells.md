Sequence Cells (_Diavolos strepitator_) are a type of obstreperous Devil.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The body of a Sequence Cell is composed entirely out of sound waves. They have no set size, although their average adult size is ~1.2 m in diameter, and as small as 10 cm in diameter. They have no upper limit. While they themselves are invisible, their outline can be seen through the anchor objects around them.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells are very primitive organisms, operating mostly out on reaction to outside stimuli. Most are not even all that difficult to kill, the real difficulty of a as Sequence Cells is their extreme reproductive speed. They can be easily notices from a distance by their unique sound they always emit and of the anchors around their bodies as they move, and their presence creates patterns in dust and sand.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells must bind themselves to solid objects for their form to remain stable. These anchor objects will then be used as amplifiers, incorporated into a shifting pattern formed around the Sequence Cell 'body'. These objects are usually similar or preferably even identical to each other, and their movement and patterns are determined by the individual Sequence Cell and their activity. The number and complexity of these anchors scales with the Sequence Cell's size.

Sequence Cells interact with their environment by manipulating sound, creating pressure differentials and kinetic force to manipulate objects or to move themselves. They can manipulate their size, capable of expanding or shrinking to 1.5x their size when necessary. They sense their environment though sound and temperature, as temperature modifies the way sound waves moves though a medium.

They can absorb objects from their surroundings into themselves and apply a partial amplifier effect to them, enforcing a similar patterned arrangement to the objects and using to defend itself or to attack others. These constructs have a very limited life span, and their creation doesn't impact the Sequence Cell itself.

Killing a Sequence Cell requires either overpowering its sound by creating an even louder sound source, disrupting the frequencies of its body, or by breaking its anchors and disrupting the pattern, without which it cannot maintain its shape and dies. Consuming [[blood]] allows the Sequence Cell to reform its pattern and quickly anchor itself to other objects. They cannot pass through solid matter, but can inhabit water, inside which their abilities and strength are elevated, able of expanding their body much more without the need of any amplifiers and even surviving with less, although sound overpowering becomes a bigger threat to them. After death, red dust accumulated from the constant vibration is released from the surface of their anchors.
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells reproduce to division. When a Sequence Cell hits a jagged surface, its sound waves will be forcefully split in two, creating two smaller individuals, each inheriting half of the parent's anchors. Sequence Cells can voluntarily divide, although they never do some, and can even combine, with multiple Sequence Cells merging into a unique individual, with no upper size limit and inheriting all of the smaller Sequence Cells' anchors.
# .diet
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Sequence Cells feed on sound, specifically on rhythmic sounds. Small Sequence Cells will absorb the sound and use it to increase their size. They consume [[blood]] through acoustic atomisation.
