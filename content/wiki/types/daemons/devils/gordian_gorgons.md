Gordian Gorgons (_Diavolos chordae_) are a type of parasitic Devil.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gordian Gorgons have a radially symmetrical anatomy, possessing ~30 muscular tendrils. While the central core is only ~5 cm, each tendril extends up to 5 m. The entire body of a Gordian Gorgon is composed of a semi-gelatinous substance with properties similar to calcite.
# .behaviour
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gordian Gorgons are solitary, the only exception being very large hosts, where each controls its own region of the body. They use their host both for mobility and defence, as they cannot survive outside the host's body or change hosts, drying out and dying. They are not sapient, although they are curious beings, using their tendrils to tinker with their surroundings, and can even learn repetitive actions. While the Devil itself leaves no smell behind, the corpse of the host does.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gordian Gorgons will pilot the corpses of their hosts as a means of movement. They do this by extending their tendrils along their host's body, forcefully flexing their muscles to create movement. Some of their tendrils will emerge from the corpse's mouth, powerful enough to completely overpower a grown [[humans|human]] and used for precise interactions with their environment (as the most they can force the corpse to do is shamble and lunge at things), reproduction, attack by whipping, and sensing, using echolocation, chemical receptors and direct contact to map out their surroundings.

The host of a Gordian Gorgon hardens into a calcite-like structure, granting it increased durability while retaining flexibility. As the corpse remains in a state of suspended putrefaction, any damage done to it is permanent (unlike damage to the Devil itself, which can still consume [[blood]] to heal), and destroying large chunks of it leaves the Gordian Gorgon vulnerable or can outright kill it from exposure to the outside. Destroying the central core also kills it on the spot. The corpse of the host remains after death.
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gordian Gorgons reproduce asexually. They will stab uninfected organisms with their tendrils, injecting their eggs directly into their flesh. When the larvae hatch, they will seek and cannibalise each other, settling somewhere around the host's centre of mass before spreading. On large enough hosts, the larvae partition the body between them instead. They can only infect living hosts.

 The larvae will extend their tendrils into the host, feeding on its body. Rashes appear within hours, followed by tumorous growths and boils. The process takes 2-3 days, and can only be stopped within the first hour, by cutting out the entry wound and the tissue around it. They will reach maturity around the same time the host dies, allowing the Gordian Gorgon to fully take over the body.
 
 [[hybrids|Hybrids]] are immune to Gordian Gorgon infection, and any infection attempt will force a backlash effect from the [[clutch_beetles|Clutch Beetle]]'s nervous structure, causing excruciating pain for the Gordian Gorgon, so powerful that they will avoid that one [[hybrids|hybrid]] for the rest of its life, and the eggs that may have entered will die on the spot. [[chimeras|Chimeras]] are also partially immune, as their [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]] soul will kill the larvae, although severe rashes will still appear.
# .diet
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Gordian Gorgons are carnivores. They secrete a very powerful digestive enzyme from their tendrils, tying their prey and eating them alive. Larvae will feed on their host's flesh and [[blood]].
