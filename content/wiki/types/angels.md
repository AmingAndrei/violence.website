Angels are divine being, native to their capital, Elysium.

Angels are humanoid, typically reaching ~2 m in height. Their eyes have black sclera and individually distinct, vibrantly coloured irises, their skin is made entirely out of white porcelain, covering their undifferentiated fleshy interior, which can crack and shatter with enough force, their hair is white, and they lack noses. Some have wings, some don't. They bleed golden [[blood]], and their scars appear as gold cracks. 

Angels are sapient, and immensely powerful, with a civilisation predating the start of humanity by tens of thousands of years. They are to [[humans]] what [[humans]] are to ants, and this led to them becoming lazy, vain and petty, choosing to not interact with what they deem as lesser beings due to seeing it as too much of a hassle. Their divine nature does not make them omnipotent or invulnerable, and they can die.

Angels are made, not born, although the exact process is unclear even to them. They do not need to eat, although they can, and require [[anima|Anima]]. They are immune to the [[toxin]] and to the FLIPSIDE's negation effect. What they are not immune to is damage from anything made of, or that interacts directly with, [[anima|Anima]] (that is to say [[devils|Devils]]), and towards [[hybrids|hybrid]] [[blood]].

The arrival of the [[supreme_acts_of_violence|Supreme Acts of Violence]] and of [[devils|Devils]] brought an end to their stagnation, and caused them to retreat into hiding. Though not being one by origin, Violence Against God is an Angel by type.
