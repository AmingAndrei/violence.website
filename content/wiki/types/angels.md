Angels are god-like, humanoid being, native to their capital, Elysium.

Angels typically reach ~2 m in height. Their eyes have black sclera and different, vibrantly coloured irises, their skin is made entirely out of white porcelain, which can crack and shatter, although it is extremely durable, their hair is white, and they lack noses. While some Angels may have wings, some don't. They can bleed, having golden [[blood]], and their scars appear as gold cracks. 

Angels are sapient, and immensely powerful, physically and technologically, with a civilisation predating the start of humanity by tens of thousands of years. They are, however, very passive. While they can interact with other beings, they choose to not do so because they see it as too much a hassle, and even then view them as lessers. Their perceived status placed them in a state of complete stagnation until the arrival of the [[supreme_acts_of_violence|Supreme Acts of Violence]] and [[devils|Devils]].

Angels are essentially made, although the exact process is unclear even to them. What is clear is their immortality clause, stating that as long as at least one Angel is still alive, their type can collectively come back to life. While not originating as one, Violence Against God is considered by type an Angel.

Angels do not eat, although they can. They do, however, require [[anima|Anima]]. They are immune to the [[toxin]] and to the FLIPSIDE's negation effect.
