Angels are god-like beings.
# .anatomy
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Angels have a humanoid appearance, reaching ~2 m in height. Their eyes have black sclera and different, vibrantly coloured irises. Their skin is made entirely out of white porcelain, and can crack and shatter, although it is extremely durable. Their hair is white. While some Angels may have wings, some don't. They can bleed, having golden [[blood]], and their scars appear as gold cracks.
# .traits
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Angels are fully sapient, and immensely powerful, physically and technologically, with a civilisation predating the start of humanity by tens of thousands of years. Their capital, Elysium, is located in a layer just above base reality. They are immune to the [[toxin]] and to the FLIPSIDE's negation effect.

Angels are however very passive. While they can interact with other beings, they don't do so because they see it as too much a hassle, and even then view them with pity or disgust as lessers. Their status has placed them in a state of complete stagnation until the arrival of the [[supreme_acts_of_violence|Supreme Acts of Violence]] and [[devils|Devils]]. While not originating as one, Violence Against God is considered by type an Angel, and holds the title of _Angel of Violence_, and the role of main predator of their species.
# .reproduction
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Angels are essentially made, although the exact process is unclear even to them. What is clear is their immortality clause, stating that as long as at least one Angel is still alive, they can collectively come back to life.
# .diet
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Angels do not eat, although they can. They do, however, require [[anima|Anima]].
