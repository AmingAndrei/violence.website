Angels are god-like, humanoid being, native to their capital, Elysium.

Angels typically reach ~2 m in height. Their eyes have black sclera and individually distinct, vibrantly coloured irises, their skin is made entirely out of white porcelain, covering their undifferentiated fleshy interior, which can crack and shatter with enough force, their hair is white, and they lack noses. Some have wings, some don't. They bleed golden [[blood]], and their scars appear as gold cracks. 

Angels are sapient, and immensely powerful physically and technologically, with a civilisation predating the start of humanity by tens of thousands of years. They are to humans what humans are to ants, and this led to them becoming lazy and petty, choosing to not interact with what they deem as lesser beings due to seeing it as too much of a hassle. Their stagnation ended once the [[supreme_acts_of_violence|Supreme Acts of Violence]] and [[devils|Devils]] arrived, which caused them to go into hiding.

Angels are made, not born, although the exact process is unclear even to them. Angels do not eat, although they can. They do, however, require [[anima|Anima]]. They are immune to the [[toxin]] and to the FLIPSIDE's negation effect. What they are not immune towards is damage from anything made of, or that interacts directly with, [[anima|Anima]], and towards [[hybrids|hybrid]] [[blood]].
