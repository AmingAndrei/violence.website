The World Wide War (WWW, 28.07.92 [[timeline|AAV]]-06.08.52 [[timeline|AAV]]) was a 40 year global conflict started by Violence Against Art with the goal of birthing a [[revolutions|Revolution]].

The actual inciting event that started the war was lost to time, but it is known Violence Against Art was the one that orchestrated it. Within a few months of the start of the war in 28.07.92 [[timeline|AAV]], every single nation on [[Sol]] joined.

While it started as trench warfare, the war quickly evolved, becoming a period of massive technological progress, as nations tried to one-up each other in a constant arms-race. [[serpentine|Serpentine]] weapon fabrication became one of the most profitable ventures of the time, and research in digital computing skyrocketed, creating the basis for the [[radio_computer_network|Radio Computer Network]] and robotic automation.

In 06.08.52 [[timeline|AAV]], the global armistice was officially signed, officially ending the World Wide War. The World Authority was founded by the remaining nations with the goal of preventing and outlawing all forms of international conflict. The final death count was estimated at ~500 million. The war's goal was met in 11.11.52 [[timeline|AAV]], when Mihaela Rebreanu was born.
