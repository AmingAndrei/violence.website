The [[chalk|Chalk]] Plague (660 [[timeline|AAV]]-652 [[timeline|AAV]]) was a plague started by Violence Against Art with the goal of birthing a [[revolutions|Revolution]].

In 660 [[timeline|AAV]], Violence Against Art deployed Chiron and the [[chalk|Chalk]] on [[sol|Sol]], with Chiron's role being to maintain its spread and gather information through the [[curses|curse]]'s hosts. During this time, numerous medical advancements were developed as a way of combating the plague, albeit none could actually treat it.

In 19.05.652 [[timeline|AAV]], Rafa was born, signalling to Chiron that the plague reached its goal. Within days, all signs of the [[chalk|Chalk]] disappeared. The final death count was estimated at ~45% of [[humans|humanity]]. The end of the plague ushered in a new era of scientific and artistic advancement.
