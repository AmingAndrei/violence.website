The [[devils|Devil]] Parade (<ruby>八百万悪魔夜行<rt>デビル・パレード</rt></ruby>, 10.03.0 [[timeline|AAV]]/[[timeline|AV]], 10.03.2006 AC), also known as the [[daemons|Daemon]] Parade was an a cataclysmic event.

The Parade was triggered by Violence Against Art with the help of Mihaela Rebreanu, and resulted in Violence Against Nature, along with countless [[devils|Devils]], pouring onto the surface of [[sol|Sol]], killing everything in sight. Numerous other [[daemons|Daemons]] were also caught in the torrent and stranded onto the planet.

Other events include [[hellfire|Hellfire]] storms scorching the planet, the temperature of [[sol|Sol]] dropping globally to -20 °C, the [[toxin]] appearing, the magnetosphere disappearing, followed with the Sun and all other observable celestial bodies vanishing, with the exception of the Moon, and the appearance of [[city_zero|City Zero]] over the ruins of [[Rojachert]]. Chronologically, while the Parade only took place over a 24 hour period, the flow of time appeared to shift and twist as it went on.

By the end, the Parade killed ~99% of all life on Sol. Most surviving [[humans]] migrated to [[city_zero|City Zero]] for shelter. Mihaela came to acquire the green [[nightmare_shackles|Nightmare Shackle]] from the Furies, later using it to trap Violence Against God in the Sarcophagus, and made a deal with the Serpent to create a barrier around [[city_zero|City Zero]].
