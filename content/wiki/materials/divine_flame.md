Divine Flame is a form of living, smokeless fire.
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Divine Flame is the main component of a [[chimeras|Chimera]]'s soul, contained within their body, expelled at will.
# .properties
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Divine Flame is differentiated from other types of fire by its primary fuel source, [[anima|Anima]], allowing it to burn underwater or even inside vacuums,. As a result, extended Divine Flame expulsion can cause a damping in [[anima|Anima]] resonance by burning temporary gaps in it. This is also what prevents it from burning forever, as Divine Flame outside of a [[chimeras|Chimera]]'s body is fleeting, burning its fuel faster than it can spread. The colour of Divine Flame is not indicative of its temperature.

Divine Flame scars are completely untreatable and excruciatingly painful. Not even full body regeneration can heal these wounds, as a consequence of the [[anima|Anima]] inside the scarred tissue and its ability to absorb [[anima|Anima]] having been completely incinerated. [[devils|Devil]] tissue is the exception to this, as due to it being composed entirely of [[anima|Anima]], it chokes the Divine Flame out instead of letting it burn, and the resulting wounds are temporary, healing themselves back after a short while.
