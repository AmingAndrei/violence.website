The Shaper is an Absolute, embodying the concept of creation.
# .design
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Shaper resembles a dagger-sword, reaching ~50 cm in length. It's double-edged, has a very short cross guard, and a T-shaped pommel. Its entire surface is unevenly coated or made out of a black, smooth substance, with rainbow spots and streaks in random locations.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Shaper is capable of directly interacting with the spirit inside a being, the [[anima|Anima]] inside one's body. The Absolute needs to be physically stabbing into the being to do so. Beyond that, it's a wild card.
# .creation
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Shaper was the weapon used by ████ to carve Violence Against Nature out of himself, which allowed [[devils|Devils]] to first take shape and lead to ████ splitting into Violence Against God and Violence Against Art, cementing the creation of the [[supreme_acts_of_violence|Supreme Acts of Violence]] and transforming the weapon into an Absolute.
