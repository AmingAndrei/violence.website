The NV Deogen is an Absolute, embodying the concept of pursuit.
# .design
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The NV Deogen is an airship, reaching ~100 m in total length, with the habitable gondola reaching a length of ~30 m. A second set of smaller envelopes is connected to the gondola by structural arms, equipped with their own set of propellers. 

The bridge is ornate and filled with various clockwork and steam-powered apparatus serving as the ship's main intelligence, its central component being a giant mechanical compass. The living quarters are equipped with all sorts of amenities for long term living, all maintained and controlled by the ship's intelligence. The NV Deogen has no need for fuel.
# .abilities
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The NV Deogen can track and pursue anyone and anything, anywhere. The target lock will be indicated by its mechanical compass, and will orient the ship in a straight line toward its target. The ship can comfortably navigate at any altitude and in any climate, and will course correct itself based on its surroundings and the target's current position. Within sightline of the target, it will make itself known through its horn. The only way to stop its attempt at reaching its destination is to manually disengage it or to give it another target.
