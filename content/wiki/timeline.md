<br><a href="/wiki" class="nav-button">← WIKI</a><br><br> The [[sol|Sol]] calendar lunisolar, split into 12 months of 29 and 30 days, plus an extra day at the end of the year, extended into two days every 4 years. Week length is 7 days, day length is 24 hours, hours are 60 minutes and minutes are 60 seconds.

The currently used eras are Ante Anno Violentiae (AAV) and Anno Violentiae (AV), used before and after the [[devil_parade|Parade]] respectively. The previously used eras were Ante Aetatem Communem (AAC) and Anno Chromae (AC), marking before and after the creation of the Oracle Machine.
