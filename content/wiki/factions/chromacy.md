The Chromacy is the spiritual power of City Zero, ruling alongside the [[pale|Pale]] and [[ultraviolet|Ultraviolet]].
# .purpose
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The Chromacy's role is to act as ruling authority over Chromaticism, its doctrine, and its followers, called chromaticans. It is their primary goal to guide their followers onto 'the Golden Path', a future constantly monitored with the help of the Oracle Machine.
# .structure
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

The supreme authority and object of worship of Chromaticism is Fair Lady Chroma. It is believed She is a a messianic figure, a physical manifestation of the world, and is the one there for us at the beginning of our lives and waiting for us at end. In reality, Lady Chroma is a corrupted interpretation of Violence Against Nature, the recollection of an encounter twisted through the years. None of the parties involved know this.

The Oracle Machine is a device from ~2000 AAV, built with the intention of analysing all possible futures of [[humans|humanity]], thought to be capable of directly communicating with Lady Chroma. There has only ever existed one Oracle Machine, being repaired and upgraded through the ages with the latest technological advancements to improve the accuracy of its readings. The Chromacy uses the Oracle Machine to analyse the collective future of [[humans|humanity]], and with it follow the 'Golden Path' set forth by Lady Chroma. In reality, the Oracle Machine analyses [[anima|Anima]] and the outputs the many extrapolated future evolutions of [[humans|humanity]] found in it.

The Chromacy is collectively administered by 10 Conductors, residing in the Choir Cathedral, being responsible for actually controlling the way Chromaticism is run and taught. Among them one is appointed as Great Conductor, an honorary position mostly, acting as the mouthpiece for the Chromacy, although having no more authority than the other conductors. They are together responsible for the interpretation of the Oracle Machine and maintaining the 'Golden Path'.

Conductors are chosen through vote, with precentors placing the name of another precentor into the Oracle Machine, which will then elect the new Conductor. The Great Conductor is chosen similarly, with Conductors voting whenever the current Great Conductor dies. Precentors are below Conductors, administering the churches as a whole, and cantors are below precentors, interacting with followers directly. The overwhelming majority of City Zero's population is composed of chromaticans.
# .conduct
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Chromaticism believes the spiritual and physical worlds are one, and when we die, we remain here to help our closest when in need, as long as our help is needed. When we're done, we peacefully cease to be, to make room for new people. [[humans|Humanity]] is no less divine than it is ordinary.

Chromaticism doesn't really believe in the concept of sin. Instead, every person is born pure, although immoral and impure thoughts and actions can tarnish the soul, which can be undone by righting your wrongs and doing more good than cumulative bad. Generally, actions that are in the interest of only oneself, that are solely in the interest of others, or that are detrimental to oneself or to others, are seen as wrong. There exist two unforgivable sins: living an unfulfilling life, and dying an unfulfilling death. Sinners cease to be instantly, and their absence can cause the souls of others to tarnish.

Prayer is separated into quiet prayer, which we do throughout our lives whenever he hope or dream for something, and loud prayer, which is when we attempt to commune directly with Lady Chroma. After death, the body is incinerated, since we now resides within the ones closest, and since death and grief can tarnish the soul. Marriage is an essential part of life, where two individuals join together for life, regardless of gender. Initiation is nigh impossible, with almost all chromaticans being born one. [[hybrids|Hybrids]] and [[fae|Fae]] half-breeds are proscribed from joining or are excommunicated.
