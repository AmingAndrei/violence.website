Hellfire is a black, iridescent flame, a form of living, smokeless fire.
# .source
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Hellfire appears to be a [[devils|Devil]] exclusive, and has so far only been created by Violence Against Nature as a more powerful version of [[Phlogiston]], made entirely out of [[Anima]]. 
# .properties
<div class="bestiary-divider"></div>

Hellfire shares all of the abilities of [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]], albeit with much more powerful upsides, such as stronger intensity, both in standard and unfavourable conditions, such as underwater and in vacuums. Hellfire burning will also not leave any noticeable gaps in [[Anima]]. Wounds are similarly untreatable, albeit they are much more grievous and dangerous, even for [[Devils]], which take longer to heal. In a direct confrontation between Hellfire and [[phlogiston|Phlogiston]], Hellfire will always overpower and choke [[Phlogiston]] out completely.
